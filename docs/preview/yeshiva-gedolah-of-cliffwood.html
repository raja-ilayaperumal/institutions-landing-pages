The mission of Yeshiva Gedolah of Cliffwood is to educate students in the systematic study and analysis of the Talmud and classical Talmudic commentaries, teach language and reasoning skills, legal research, logic and modes of rational argument, emphasize the moral application of Talmudic principles through the study of Mussar and ethical works, convey an integrated Torah perspective for meaningful and productive interaction within contemporary society, and provide a background in Halacha with an emphasis in practical application.
